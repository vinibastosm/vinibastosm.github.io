About

I am a PhD student in Experimental and Theoretical Physics at the São Carlos Institute of Physics (IFSC-USP), funded by FAPESP, working in the Nanoplasmonics Group (CePOF) under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Euclydes Marega Junior. I hold a B.Sc. in Computational Physics from IFSC-USP, where I used the Finite-Difference Time-Domain (FDTD) method to study the optical properties of plasmonic structures.

My research sits at the intersection of nanophotonics and scientific machine learning. I combine electromagnetic simulations with Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) and Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for the modeling and inverse design of nanophotonic devices, with applications in strong coupling, polaritonic systems and two-dimensional (2D) materials.
